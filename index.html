Who Can Donate Blood?
  • Age Requirement: Between 18 and 65 years old.
  • Weight: At least 50 kg (110 lbs) with good overall health.
  • Donation Interval: At least 84 days (3 months) since your last whole blood donation.
  • Hemoglobin Level: Minimum 12.5 g/dL measured before donation.
  • Hydration & Rest: Drink plenty of fluids and have a healthy meal before donating.
